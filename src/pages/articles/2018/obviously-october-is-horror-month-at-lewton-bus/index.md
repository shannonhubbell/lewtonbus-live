Are you ready for some screams and chills?  
**_Lewton Bus_** is celebrating Horror Month, and we've got quite a lineup for you. We're going to look at family-friendly horror, the feminism of a good witch story, and the design of **_Hannibal_**'s memory palaces as a landscape of gothic horror. We'll also bring horror-themed favorites, like a special edition of Stream of Consciousness. We'll also have reviews of new horror movies and TV shows, like the new _**Halloween**_ and the _**Chilling Adventures of Sabrina**_.

Stay tuned for more high quality horror-tainment!
