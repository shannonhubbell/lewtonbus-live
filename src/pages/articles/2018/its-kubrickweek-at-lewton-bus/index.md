Join us this week, on the 50th anniversary of the towering science fiction classic _**2001: A Space Odyssey**_, for a celebration of the work of Stanley Kubrick! He is the favorite film artist of a few of the Lewton Bus crew, myself included, and we're excited to share our insights into his often challenging, always rewarding filmography.  


  
  


  

To see all of our pieces on Kubrick this week, check out [KubrickWeek](http://lewtonbus.net/tag/kubrickweek/).
