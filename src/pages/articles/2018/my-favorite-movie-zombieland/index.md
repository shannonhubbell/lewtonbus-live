We've got more [My Favorite Movie](http://lewtonbus.net/tag/my-favorite-movie/), Horror Month edition! This episode, we are joined by [Sthefany "Z" Flores](http://lewtonbus.net/author/z-flores/), to talk about _**Zombieland**__,_ the Ruben Fleischer ([_**Venom**_](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-venom/))directed_**,**_ Rhett Reese and Paul Wernick ([_**Deadpool**_ **_2_**](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-deadpool-2/)) penned horror comedy_._ We talk about Metallica, my co-host Allen's obsession with punchable faces (to the point of having one), and exceptional amounts of bloody vomit.\[note\]Pretty sure every zombie in this film was [poisoned by their constituents](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kJH-5G9NqHw).\[/note\]
