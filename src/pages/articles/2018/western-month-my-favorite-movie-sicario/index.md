My Favorite Movie is back! After (another) hiatus, we're back with another episode. Now that we're in the final week of [Western Month](http://lewtonbus.net/tag/western-month/), we are joined by [Will Hyland](http://lewtonbus.net/author/will-hyland/), one of our contributors here to talk about the [Taylor Sheridan](http://lewtonbus.net/editorials/western-month-the-subversive-work-of-taylor-sheridan/)\-penned and Denis Villeneuve-directed neo-Western _**Sicario**__._ Join us as we discuss the War on Drugs, the works of Cormac McCarthy, and Will's weird stimulation via bleak cinema.
