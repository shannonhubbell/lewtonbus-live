I'm extremely proud to present this latest episode of [The Cutting Room](http://lewtonbus.net/tag/the-cutting-room/), the official horror podcast of **Lewton Bus**. I mean, I'm always extremely proud, because I have such a blast talking bloody shop with Ryan and Tanner, but this is a special one. This time around we're joined by a close buddy of mine: Jason Horton. Jason is a prolific independent filmmaker, and his most recent film, **_The Campus_****,** [can be found on Amazon Video right now](https://www.amazon.com/Campus-Rachel-Amanda-Bryant/dp/B07938TLQ1/ref=sr_1_1?ie=UTF8&qid=1519772142&sr=8-1&keywords=the+campus). Check it out!

I really think you'll like this episode. We dig into everything from the creative process behind **_The Campus_** to Jason's business model to his (perhaps unexpected) experiences in non-horror filmmaking. Enjoy!

On a more personal note, the **Lewton Bus** crew in general and The Cutting Room hosts in specific would like to dedicate this episode to our recently departed friend, [Ian Abbott](http://lewtonbus.net/in-memoriam/in-memoriam-ian-abbott/).

Ride eternal, Ian.
