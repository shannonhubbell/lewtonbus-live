Simone Chavoor's [**_Die Hard With a Podcast_**](http://lewtonbus.net/tag/die-hard-with-a-podcast/) returns! This time she's addressing one of history's greatest debates: is **_Die Hard_** a Christmas movie? Take a listen!

> If you’re going to talk Die Hard, you cannot avoid the meme/debate over whether or not it’s a Christmas movie. We discuss why it is (and okay, a few reasons why not), but more importantly, _why_ we care about the question in the first place. What does Christmas mean to us, and how do pop culture traditions factor in?
