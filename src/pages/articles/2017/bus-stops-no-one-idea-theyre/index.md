Hello everyone!\[note\]I've decided to steal [Karl Hass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Haas)' famous catchphrase for my podcast intros.\[/note\] In this mildly digression-prone episode of **_The Bus Stops Here_**, [Adam](http://lewtonbus.net/author/adam-bumas/), [Ryan](http://lewtonbus.net/author/ryan-roch/) and I talk about Lucasfilm's dumping of Colin Trevorrow Lucasfilm and Colin Trevorrow deciding to see other people, the new film adaptation of Steven King's **_It_**, and awesome trains in science fiction movies. Other nerdy stuff, too. It was fun to record. Here are the [Pennywise design article](http://ew.com/article/2016/08/16/pennywise-costume-stephen-king-it-movie/) that Ryan mentions and the [Millennium Falcon article](https://kitbashed.com/blog/a-complete-history-of-the-millennium-falcon) I reference.

Enjoy!
