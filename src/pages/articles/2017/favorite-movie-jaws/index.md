The Bus Stops Here is back after a Thanksgiving break with a new episode of _**My Favorite Movie**__._  This month, Allen and Kevin are joined by Lewton Bus's own [Jared Eves](http://lewtonbus.net/author/jared-eves/) to talk about his favorite film, the original summer megablockbuster, Steven Spielberg's _**Jaws**_! As long in the tooth as it is, the film is no less incisive today than the day it chewed through the box office.
