As you might have noticed the Lewton Bus crew are pretty big fans of [James Bond](http://lewtonbus.net/tag/does-it-look-like-its-our-first-time/), so it's safe to say that we've been following the last few years of will-he-or-won't-he surrounding Daniel Craig's possible return to the franchise with a curious eye. And during that time it's been difficult to predict exactly what Baccarat (or Poker, I guess) hand we might be dealt. Until now.

Finally confirming his return last night on Stephen Colbert (much to the chagrin of his other interviewers that day, I imagine), Bond fans both breathed a sigh of relief, and held their collective breaths, impossible as it may sound. It's no secret that Craig's last outing was... less than good\[note\]I'm being charitable - it was, in fact, one of the worst entries in the entire franchise, and we all know it.\[/note\], and it's hard to not wince at the thought of another misfire on such a colossal level, but it's also an opportunity for him to go out on a high note. And as Bond fans - and fans of Craig's iteration of the super spy in general - we can't be happier to have that chance.

Now onto the next order of speculative business: who will direct?
