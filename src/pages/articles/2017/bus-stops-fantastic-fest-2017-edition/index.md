For this Very Special Episode™ of The Bus Stops Here, I was joined by Jon Hansen, Jason DeWall, Cassady Toles and Dwayne Allen for a round table discussion of what we've seen so far at Fantastic Fest 2017. It's the first time we've recorded an episode _with_ _people in the same room_, and it was a lot of fun. Enjoy!
