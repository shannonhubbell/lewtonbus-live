Well, we've reached the end of August and that means the Summer of blockbusters is officially over.  With awards season and the new trend of fall/winter blockbusters on the horizon, [Allen](http://lewtonbus.net/author/allen-strickland/), [Ryan](http://lewtonbus.net/author/ryan-roch/), [Shannon](http://lewtonbus.net/author/shannon-hubbell/) and myself chatted about the best (obviously [_**The Book of Henry**_](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-book-henry/)) and worst this summer had to offer.  Plus, our first foray into listener questions!  Huzzah!

You can listen through Soundcloud, or through any of the links below.  Please like, subscribe and share with all of your movie-loving friends!  And for those of you who listened, who do you think has the handsomest voice of the Lewton Bus crew?
