Stop your grinnin' and drop your linen!  
This Friday, Ridley Scott's **_Alien: Covenant_** will burst bloodily forth from theater screens. In anticipation of this latest installment in everybody's favorite body/cosmic horror science fiction franchise, the **_Lewton Bus_** crew is devoting this week to **_Alien_**. We're talking articles, an episode of **_The Bus Stops Here_** (the **_Lewton Bus_** podcast, which you _should_ be listening to), and our own review of the new movie. At the end of the week, I look forward to joining you all for **_Covenant_**\-related discussion in our now-traditional Spoilerdome thread.

Stay tuned! This is sure to be a lot of fun.  


  
We might even wake up Hicks.
