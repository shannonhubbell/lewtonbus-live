This is Hello Wizard speaking., although my real name is Shaun Higgins. I'm a self-taught video editor and am very grateful to be on Lewton Bus.

Back in 2015, I made the decision to analyse the film-making techniques of every movie Steven Soderbergh had made up to his retirement. The series is still ongoing, but to celebrate the release of Soderbergh's newest film **_Logan Lucky_**, I will be posting my episodes here on Lewton Bus. To kick things off, we might as well start at where it all began by taking a look at his Palme d'Or-winning debut feature, _**Sex, Lies and Videotape**_.

So cover your windows with bedsheets, drink some ice tea with lemon and enjoy.
