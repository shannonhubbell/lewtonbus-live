This week on the Essence of Camp, Dave and Emmett break down (and have a breakdown) the "the Mount Everest of insane '70s Italian movies", 1979's **_The Visitor_**. Dave discusses his favorite brutalist architecture of the 1970's, Emmett goes all in on the killer kid genre, and director and all around bad dad John Huston (not really) joins the show to warn us of a very evil 8-year old named Kay Collins, and how she wants to bring about the end of the world.
