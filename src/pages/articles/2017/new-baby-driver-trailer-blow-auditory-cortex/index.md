I am _not_ a patient man. It's been too long since we've gotten a new motion picture out of the brilliant Edgar Wright.  


  
  


  
 **_The World's End_** was way back in 2013 (Did we even have cellphones back then?) and in the intervening years we saw him jump ship from Marvel's **_Ant-Man_**. I need my regular dose of character-centric, extremely well-crafted comedic genre cinema, so I am beyond pumped that **_Baby Driver_** is almost upon us. Sure, I think it's weird that the material he chose was a **_Boss Baby_** spinoff about Alec Baldwin's infant limousine driver, but I'll take what I can get. Let's check out the new trailer!

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YirEgK7yJCg

Huh. Well, that's what I get for making assumptions. It turns out **_Baby Driver_** is a (musical) heist film centered around a getaway driver! Boy, do I feel foolish. But, man, this trailer's got the goods.  


  
With its kinetic visual style, humor, and pop cultural awareness, it feels as Edgar Wright-y as a trailer can get without involving a Cornetto somehow. And that cast! I can't wait.

What are your thoughts? Are you in lesbians with this trailer?
