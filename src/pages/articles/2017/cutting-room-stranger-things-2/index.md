For the latest episode of **_Lewton Bus_**' horror podcast, [Allen](http://lewtonbus.net/author/allen-strickland), [Ryan](http://lewtonbus.net/author/ryan-roch/) and I had a freewheeling discussion about the Duffer Brothers' love letter to 1980s genre cinema, _**Stranger Things 2**_. We talked about what a great name Finn Wolfhard is, how cool Steve is all of a sudden, and what we want out of season three.

We did not talk about Barb. You're welcome.
