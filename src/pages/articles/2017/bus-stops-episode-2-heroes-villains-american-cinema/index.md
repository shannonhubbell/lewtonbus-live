Take that, naysayers! We've made it to episode _two_ of [The Bus Stops Here](http://lewtonbus.net/tag/the-bus-stops-here/), our homegrown **_Lewton Bus_** podcast. This time around, [Tanner](http://lewtonbus.net/author/tanner-volz/), [Adam](http://lewtonbus.net/author/adam-pilfold-bagwell/) and [I](http://lewtonbus.net/author/shannon-hubbell/) got together for a discussion that wanders through the superhero genre, _**Die Hard**,_ the films of Paul Verhoeven, and many other topics. It was a blast, and we hope you enjoy the listen.
