I'm in it for life with Guillermo del Toro.  
Even his less beloved films (_**Pacific Rim**_ and, weirdly, the sumptuous _**Crimson Peak**_) are gorgeous, hypnotic affairs. It appears that _**The Shape of Water**_ is no exception - I mean, look at this new trailer! He loves his monsters!

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XFYWazblaUA

Vaguely threatening Michael Shannon is a wonderful thing. The story appears to feature a bizarro sort of love story with Doug Jones back in deep cover as a creepy blend of Abe Sapien and the Creature from the Black Lagoon.  


  
It just doesn't get much more Guillermo del Toro than this.  


  
I for one can't wait.

Some of the Lewton Bus gang also observed recently this new trend of cutting trailers to musical rhythms.  
  


  
It's obvious and a little silly, but a step up from the years-old convention of dropping terrible power ballads into trailers.
