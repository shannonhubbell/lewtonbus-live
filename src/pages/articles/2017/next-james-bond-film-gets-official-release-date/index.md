Well folks, after an unusually long wait, it's finally happened. We have the release date for the next James Bond film.  


  
  


  
The official twitter account for everyone's favorite cinematic super spy has announced that the character will be returning to theaters November 8th 2019.

https://twitter.com/007/status/889575964983914496

So what does this mean? Well honestly right now all it means is that we now know the next Bond film is dated for Fall 2019. We currently do not know the title, plot, director, or even who will be portraying the character. My guess is that Daniel Craig will be returning for a final outing, but with his past comments  on the prospect there is absolutely no telling.  


  
That means it is time for wild speculation!  


  
Personally I'm very interested in getting someone like Susanne Bier (_**The Night Manager**_) to direct a _**Fast and the Furious**_ or _**John Wick**_ inspired Bond film, allowing Craig to fully maximize his talents in the role one more time.
