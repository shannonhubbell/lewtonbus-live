With COVID-19 impacting every other aspect of society and our lives, it only makes sense that the movie industry would be as well. With the impacts both immediate and long term there’s a lot to discuss, so we convened a special episode of The Bus Stops Here to talk it out.

Join us as [Allen](https://lewtonbus.net/author/allen-strickland/), [Adam](https://lewtonbus.net/author/adam-bumas/), and [Shannon](https://lewtonbus.net/author/shannon-hubbell) dig into the impact of COVID-19 on the film industry, and a spirited debate about the ages of the stars of **_Supernatural_**.
