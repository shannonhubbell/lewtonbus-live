The year 2020 has been redeemed! After last week's death march of a "Christmas movie,"\[note\]My fault. That was one of my picks. I've been self-flagellating since then, and not in the sexy way.\[/note\] we've turned it around with a genuinely good Christmas movie. That's right, after forty damned episodes we've finally gotten around to Charles Band's sci-fi action opus _**Trancers**_. Thank you, Ryan, for choosing this for your reload.

https://soundcloud.com/thebusstopshere/cinema-chance-cube-ep-40-charles-band-i-love-you?ref=clipboard&p=a&c=1

Long story short, this movie is an underappreciated gem of genre cinema, Charles Band is amazing, and we don't deserve Tim Thomerson.

\[caption id="attachment\_15148" align="aligncenter" width="499"\]![Photo of actors Tim Thomerson and Helen Hunt](https://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/5fdbc80aa2d8c.image_.jpg) Tim Thomerson (left)\[/caption\]

We had a blast watching this movie _and_ a blast recording this show, like the ethereal union of two lost souls. This is retroactively our Christmas episode, so revel in the Santa-slaughtering holiday cheer, but don't forget to subscribe, rate and review!
