The Cinema Chance Cube returns! Well, not really. We were never really gone, but we've been behind on sharing these episodes on _Lewton Bus_ itself. If you only follow the show through this site, your cup runneth over! See the embeds below.

In this episode, the boys discuss [Michael Crichton](https://lewtonbus.net/podcasts/cinema-chance-cube-the-great-train-robbery-and-matinee/)'s 1984 science fiction thriller, _**Runaway**_. Starring Tom Selleck, Tom Selleck's mustache, and Gene Simmons, this movie is a blast. It features a Roomba with a handgun. How can you go wrong? We also have a surprise quiz near the end that is surprisingly hard. Don't worry if you don't do well. I know we basically shat the bed.

Listen in!

https://soundcloud.com/thebusstopshere/cinema-chance-cube-ep-37-gene-simmons-is-a-kaiju

The other choices that Fate rejected were the 1987 Rutger Hauer action vehicle _**Wanted: Dead or Alive**_, the 1995 Kaiju classic **_Gamera: Guardian of the Universe_**, _another_ Kaiju classic, 2001's _**Godzilla, Mothra and King Ghidorah: Giant Monsters All-Out Attack**_, the 1971 feature film debut of forgotten b-movie hack Steven Spielberg, **_Duel_**, and the 1968 original **_Planet of the Apes_**, which features a planet with apes on it.

All of those films would have been great choices, but I'm very happy with what we got. This is a fun episode.
