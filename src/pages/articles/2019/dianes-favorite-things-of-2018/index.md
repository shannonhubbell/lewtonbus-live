2018 was a wild adventure that veered from amazing to horrifically depressing and nightmarish. By this, I mean obviously, the existence of [_**Gotti**_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fONiyOcdrCA). Let's not dwell on that. Without further distraction, here are my favorite TV shows, movies, and games of 2018.

* * *

## _**Crazy Rich Asians**_

![](http://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Constance-Wu-crazy-rich-asians.jpg)

I saw this movie so many times, I actually forgot how many times I’ve actually seen it. It was at least three times. Four? Anyway, the last time I saw this many Asian faces was - well, my own family gatherings - and watching **_Crazy Rich Asians_** ([read my review](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-crazy-rich-asians/)) was sort of like being around my family. East Asians have endured a great deal as people; from the diaspora, to family secrets and discussions too painful to have decades later, and the warmth of being together and supporting each other. _Ours_ is a universal story, too, and finally, we are seen.

* * *

## **_First Reformed_**

What do we owe this planet when we have decimated nature and made it a husk of itself? Have turned our backs to God? What do we have left, if not God? These are [central themes](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/phoenix-film-festival-review-first-reformed/) in _**First Reformed**_, which has been in my brain ever since I saw it. It is Paul Schrader’s angriest screed against the follies of humanity, and Ethan Hawke delivers perhaps the best performance of his entire career. It is an incredible movie that asks big questions through the eyes of one very personal story. These are questions we need to be asking ourselves. Let’s just hope it isn’t too late.

* * *

## **_Aquaman_**

![](http://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/jason-momoa-aquaman-movie.jpg)

Arthur Curry talks to fish! There’s an octopus playing the drums! There’s a whole kingdom of _crab royalty_! OCEAN MASTER! BLACK MANTA! **_Aquaman_** is [pure delight](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-aquaman/). Patrick Wilson brings an immense amount of gravitas to his role as Orm, Arthur’s brother and King of Atlantis. The movie also refreshingly rejects toxic masculinity even as it presents us a hero whose greatest physical feature is his muscles. Jason Momoa is fantastic as Arthur, a would-be king who doesn’t know if he can lead a kingdom because despite his special abilities, he’s also just also a mere man (but not a merman). That’s the beauty of this movie. Special abilities and birthright don’t make us destined for greatness; we have to work hard to achieve great things, sometimes with a little help from our underwater friends.

* * *

## **_Schitt’s Creek_**

![](http://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Schitts-Creek.jpg)

Hello, you big, dirty raccoons. **_Schitt’s Creek_**, a Canadian comedy about a family of rich doofuses who lose their money and move to a small town only to find even more doofuses, is one of the most heartwarming shows on TV right now. The Rose family is good, even if they’re set in their ways. They aren’t schemers, they’re just survivors and everyone in their tiny town is surviving alongside them, in their own way. The show’s secret weapon is Dan Levy. As co-creator (alongside his father, Eugene Levy, who also plays John), actor (he plays David), and showrunner, Levy has immense control over what the show looks like. By showing that David, a pansexual man, is accepted and cared for in this small town, **_Schitt’s Creek_** elects to reject small town stereotypes and is mindful that depicting a full life doesn’t always have to mean depicting pain and homophobia. Also, please enjoy this [supercut of Alexis saying “David.”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jC5gqHxjzh8&t=36s)

* * *

## **_Venom_**

By a miracle called Tom Hardy, [this movie works](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-venom/). Despite its editing and plot flaws, it manages to be funny and charming. Michelle Williams is fantastic, Tom Hardy is evolving before our eyes into the kind of bizarre character actor he’s always meant to be, and it also features an actual, heartwarming message of two unlikely heroes (seriously, they’re both kind of bad at _everything_) becoming friends and adults who act like adults when faced with heartbreak and betrayal.

* * *

## **_The Rider_**

![](http://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/rider-without-horse.jpg)

I’ve [talked](http://lewtonbus.net/criticism/redefining-masculinity-rider/) [about](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-the-rider/) this movie for what seems like a decade, so I’m committed to finishing the year strong and yet again recommending this incredible film. I keep thinking about those incredible South Dakota landscapes shot at the golden hour and the story of a cowboy who’s forced to adapt when he can’t readily fix his broken body. I’ve recommended it to everyone I know, and there’s no way anyone regrets seeing this movie.

* * *

## **_Annihilation_**

Around the time you hear the howling wail of a bear - or is it a person? - you’ll want to hide under some covers. Though [**_Annihilation_**](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-annihilation/) is nowhere as scary as some critics made it out to be, it is truly unsettling. It’s a surreal science fiction story, meant to upend your idea of what is real and what isn’t, what is invading our planet and what it wants. It reminded me of **_Arrival_** in some ways, asking us what we are willing to do to survive.

* * *

## **_The Chilling Adventures of Sabrina_**

![](http://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/Sabrina.jpg)

Since this will run during the holiday season, it’s appropriate to wish all of you a blessed morning, and I hope that none of you had your throats slit by malevolencies during the night. It’s quotes like that that imbue _**The Chilling Adventures of Sabrina**_ with its [spooky magic](http://lewtonbus.net/television/review-the-chilling-adventures-of-sabrina-episode-1/), a pulpy show rich with color and texture and a fantastic set of leads in Sabrina, Zelda, Hilda, Ambrose, and Harvey.

* * *

## **_Black Panther_**

To say this was [highly anticipated](http://lewtonbus.net/reviews/black-panther-review-powerful-new-element-mcu-formula/) is an understatement. When February 2018 rolled around, the entire country (and the world) was buzzing with excitement to see Wakanda for the first time and a nearly all-black cast. What we got was an epic mash-up of cultures, Wakandan and American, anchored by strong performances, memorable designs, and a sense that the MCU had changed for the better with such a [rich new foray into Wakanda](http://lewtonbus.net/editorials/seeing-your-culture-as-an-outsider-and-black-panther/).

* * *

## _**The Magicians**_

![](http://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Magicians.gif)

A few days ago, Netflix added season 3 of this wonderfully bonkers SyFy Original show, and I couldn't be happier. Critics derisively called this 'horny Hogwarts with booze' when it began, but they were completely missing out on what was happening beneath the surface. This show, about a band of misfit students with magical abilities and no real concept of responsibility who are suddenly responsible for the fate of magic across several worlds, has hidden layers. The real gift is in the characters, who are all dealing with that responsibility in surprising (and for some, surprisingly inappropriate) ways. The true highlights of this show are Margo and Eliot, who find themselves ruling a kingdom before they've even graduated. Their grasp of diplomacy has to be swift, as they face encroachment, coups, and angry gods.
