_Lewton Bus is very proud to co-sponsor a monthly event benefiting Oakland’s [Museum of Art And Digital Entertainment](https://themade.org/). 1UPtown! A night of movies and the video games they influenced (or were based upon). This month is **Raiders of the Lost Ark**, and it’s gonna be a blast. If you’re going to be in the San Francisco Bay Area, please come down, enjoy some games, watch an adventure movie classic, and enjoy some drinks to benefit a good cause. Here are the details!_

\[divider type="thin" spacing="25"\]

Calling all retro gamers! Every 3rd Saturday 5-8 we are host to 1UPtown, a night of movies and games.

In 1977, Steven Spielberg told his buddy George Lucas that he was interested in making a James Bond movie. Lucas countered that he had a character "better than James Bond"... Indiana Smith. With a name change to Jones (after Lucas' dog), one of the greatest action adventure characters was born. In 1981, _**Raiders of the Lost Ark**_ was released. Hot take here: the movie's pretty freakin' great.

And it's had a lasting influence, including on games. Come to the Uptown Nightclub to watching the movie and play the console games the movie inspired.

This event doesn't "belong in a museum." It benefits one!

![](https://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/2019-10-raiders-lb.jpg)

\[divider type="thin" spacing="25"\]
