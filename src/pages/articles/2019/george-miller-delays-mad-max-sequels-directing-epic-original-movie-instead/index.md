[**_Deadline_** is reporting](https://deadline.com/2019/12/george-miller-mad-max-fury-road-sequel-superhero-movie-debate-start-date-three-thousand-years-of-longing-interview-1202802441/) **_Mad Max: Fury Road_** director George Miller has hopped back in the saddle and is underway on his next movie, titled **_Three Thousand Years of Longing_**. Sequels to the hit recent **_Mad Max_** installment [have been tied up in a lawsuit](https://www.cinemablend.com/news/2467715/warner-bros-wants-more-mad-max-movies-from-george-miller) with Warner Brothers over a bonus the director claims is owed to him for services rendered—and if you’ve seen **_Fury Road_**, you know those services went above and beyond the call of duty.

“Fans,” of course, wanted Miller to try to get Zack Snyder’s DCU under some sort of control, but the septuagenarian had ideas of his own and is working on bringing his new film, billed not as action oriented as **_Fury Road_**, but still epic in scope, to market. Starring Tilda Swinton and Idris Elba, **_Three Thousand Years of Longing_** is described by Miller as a two-hander, but beyond that he doesn’t want not puncture the magic. “I’ve always felt that if you talk about these films before they’re actually completed, you jinx them,” he said and it’s a feeling most creatives can recognize.  
  


**_Mad Max: Fury Road_**, from the same mind that brought us the **_Happy Feet_** movies and the **_Babe_** sequel, **_Pig in the City_**, was an action masterpiece for all time that topped the gonzo action of the original films and blew all of our collective socks off. Miller can survey us from atop that mountain of socks and we know that whatever he serves up next will be worth the price of admission.
