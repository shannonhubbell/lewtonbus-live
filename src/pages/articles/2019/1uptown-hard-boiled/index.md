Are you going to be in the San Francisco Bay Area on Saturday, August 17th, or know someone who will be? Join us at Oakland's [Uptown Nightclub](http://www.uptownnightclub.com/) for [1UPtown](https://www.facebook.com/1UPtownOakland), a night of video games and movies. This month we're screening John Woo's 1992 action masterpiece **_Hard Boiled_**. Starring the incomparable Chow Yun-fat (**_The Killer_**, _**Crouching Tiger Hidden Dragon**_) and Tony Leung Chiu-wai (**_In the Mood For Love_**, **_Infernal Affairs_**), **_Hard Boiled_** is one of the most exhilarating and influential action films of all time.

It also features a newborn baby saving Chow Yun-fat's life, and why would you want to miss that?

While **_Hard Boiled_** is screening, play some of the video games that it's inspired over the years!

\[divider type="thin" spacing="25"\]

1UPtown is presented by the [Museum of Art and Digital Entertainment (MADE)](https://themade.org/), lewtonbus.net and the [Uptown Nightclub](https://themade.org/). A portion of the bar goes to benefit the museum, so you should double-fist some booze while our Hong Kong heroes double-fist guns and fly through the air in slow motion! RSVP via [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/events/402157910654814/) or [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/the-made-presents-1uptown-hard-boiled-tickets-68402590991?aff=ebdshpsearchautocomplete). It's free! Can't beat that.

\[divider type="thin" spacing="25"\]

21+ with valid ID

The Uptown is located just around the corner from the 19th Street BART Station and is wheelchair accessible. 1928 Telegraph Ave, Oakland, CA 94612.
