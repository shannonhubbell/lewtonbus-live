Prolific independent filmmaker [J. Horton](https://lewtonbus.net/tag/jason-horton/) ([**_Death Day_**](https://lewtonbus.net/reviews/the-campus-review/), **_Edges of Darkness_**, **_Trap_**) has returned with a new horror thriller, _**Craving**_. Check out the trailer!

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OnWOxTMdb9Y

Full disclosure, Horton and I have been good friends for over two decades. So keep that in mind when I say I think this looks like a blast.  
  
I am also a total sucker for [siege films](https://lewtonbus.net/criticism/western-month-the-non-western-westerns-of-john-carpenter/). Here's the synopsis.

> After a drug deal goes south, four heroin addicts barricade themselves in a country bar as the cops close in.  
>   
> 
> 
>   
> Withdrawal sets in, further complicating their hostage situation, while a secret one of them is hiding could destroy them all.  
> 
>

[**_Craving_**](https://www.thejhorton.com/craving) hits Amazon, Google Play, and Vudu on March 8th.
