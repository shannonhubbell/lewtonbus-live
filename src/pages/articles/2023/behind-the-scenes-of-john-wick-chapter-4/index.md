[I am on the record](https://lewtonbus.net/editorials/shannons-top-25-movies-of-the-2010s/) as being a big [John Wick](https://lewtonbus.net/tag/john-wick/) fan, so consider me jazzed about this behind the scenes video for **_John Wick: Chapter 4_**. Is it just corporate marketing? Yes, but I am a simple man and this stuff works on me. [Check it out.](https://twitter.com/JohnWickMovie/status/1628086867135709185?t=wZCcAYFk9o4rgQu7O6wkSA&s=19)

https://twitter.com/JohnWickMovie/status/1628086867135709185?t=wZCcAYFk9o4rgQu7O6wkSA&s=19

![](https://lewtonbus.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Screenshot-2023-02-21-at-12.21.48-PM.jpg)No stolen [horses](https://lewtonbus.net/reviews/review-john-wick-chapter-3-parabellum/) this time. Bummer. But hey, at least the Velociraptors dogs are back! So are a literal murderer's row of franchise faves with Lance Reddick, Laurence Fishburne, and of course Ian "Cocksucker" McShane returning. There are also a bunch of great new faces, including one that I am particularly pumped about: martial arts b-picture badass Scott Adkins. Wearing a fat suit!

But please, what we're really here for is The World's Best Boy [Keanu Reeves](https://lewtonbus.net/tag/keanu-reeves/) murdering the living heck out of hordes of goons with a variety of weapons (Including nunchucks!) against the backdrop of an ornate, stylishly shot demimonde of assassins. Headshots! Fights! Stunts! Huzzah! Looks like Reeves and director Chad Stahelski intend to deliver.

_**John Wick: Chapter 4**_ will be in theaters March 24th.
