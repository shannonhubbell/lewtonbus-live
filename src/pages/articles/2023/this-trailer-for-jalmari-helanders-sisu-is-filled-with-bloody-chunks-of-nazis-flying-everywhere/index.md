In case revisiting _**Raiders of the Lost Ark**_, _**Inglorious Basterds**_, and _**Green Room**_ isn't filling your "fuck yeah Nazi death" urges, it looks like director Jalmari Helander (_**Rare Exports: A Christmas Tale**_, _**Big Game**_) has you covered. The film is set during the Lapland War, and centers around a Finish gold hunter taking on a squad of Nazi assholes.  
  


  
This involves blood. Lots and lots of blood. Be warned that this is a red band trailer, so if you're not up for some Nazi dismemberment don't click.  
  


  

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xqNo0oMW5Nw

It's been a minute, but I remember quite enjoying Helander's **_Rare Exports_****,** and this trailer is a hoot. Some well crafted, cathartic slaughter of fascists sounds exactly like what the doctor ordered.  

  

_**Sisu**_ will be released in the US on April 28th.
