It's been about a month since I turned [the most important age of all](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phrases_from_The_Hitchhiker%27s_Guide_to_the_Galaxy#Answer_to_the_Ultimate_Question_of_Life,_the_Universe,_and_Everything_\(42\)), so for our latest [Cinema Chance Cube](https://lewtonbus.net/tag/cinema-chance-cube/) episode the other boys bizarrely agreed to let me stack the Cube entirely with my own choices. Me being me I picked a bunch of goofy genre pictures, mostly from the 1990s. I have a type. Here are my choices:

- Renny Harlin's 1999 mega mako shark masterpiece, **_Deep Blue Sea_**.
- **_Army of Darkness_**, Sam Raimi's 1992 conclusion to his splatstick **_Evil Dead_** trilogy.
- _**Blade**_ director Stephen Norrington's 1994 debut **_Death_ _Machine_**, featuring Brad Dourif and his pet killer robot.
- Stephen Sommers pits Treat Williams and (sigh) Famke Janssen against an army of tentacle monsters in the 1998 creature feature **_Deep Rising_****.**
- Peter Jackson, who went on to do nothing else with his career, made the insanely gory 1992 zombie comedy **_Dead Alive_****.**
- One-non 90s movie! **_Guardians of the Galaxy_** director James Gunns' 2006 body horror comedy **_Slither_****!**

And what movie did fate choose? **_Deep Rising_****!** This movie is a hoot, and we enjoyed the hell out of recording this episode. Happy birthday to me!
