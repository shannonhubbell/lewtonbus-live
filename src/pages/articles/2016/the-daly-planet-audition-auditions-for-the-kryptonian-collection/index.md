I recently had the pleasure of joining [Scott Daly](https://twitter.com/scottdaly85), [Matt Freeman](https://twitter.com/mordinamael) and [Paul Acevedo](https://twitter.com/PaulRAcevedo) on The Daly Planet podcast for a discussion of Takashi Miike's 1999 horror film, _**Audition**_. [Check it out!](http://dalyplanetfilms.com/2016/12/14/the-kryptonian-collection-episode-7-audition/)

> It’s time for another episode of [The Kryptonian Collection](http://dalyplanetfilms.com/the-kryptonian-collection/), the monthly series where we discuss, debate, and eventually vote on a film’s entrance into the illustrious Kryptonian Collection – a list of the finest films our planet has to offer.
> 
> This month, gaming journalist Paul Acevedo stops by to put forth the 1999 Japanese horror film **_Audition_** for consideration. Will **_Audition_** meet the standard of our list, or will we be forced to stab it in the eyes with pointy needles? Tune in and find out!
