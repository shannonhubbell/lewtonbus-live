Schlocktober continues on the [_**Essence of Camp**_](https://soundcloud.com/essence-of-camp), as we tackle Clive Barker's 1987 directorial debut **_Hellraiser_**. In this episode, David discusses the finer points of Spanish horror, I try (and fail) to Monday morning quarterback some of Barker's directorial choices, and special guest Pinhead drops by to discuss the upper reaches of camp cinema.
