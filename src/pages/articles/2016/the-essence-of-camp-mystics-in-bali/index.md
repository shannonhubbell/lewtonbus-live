This week, David and Emmett wrap up Schlocktober with the Indonesian horror classic _**Mystics in Bali**_. In this episode, David pitches a mumblecore version of _**Friday the 13th**_, Emmett gives listeners a crash course in 1980's Southeast Asian politics, and special guest the demon Pazuzu promises to perform unspeakable acts upon their mothers in Hell.
